### Ongoing Projects

### [BAS Agent](https://github.com/Omkar96-18/SIH26174_BAS_AgentByXathonerTeam) – AI Human Activity Recognition for Project SIH26174 of ISRO.
We are preparing an AI software system for project SIH26174 of ISRO targeting the problem statement of Smart India Hackathon 2026. The software will enable monitoring astronaut activities during conducting experiments at the Bharatiya Antariksh station:

>- Real-time video processing used offline to detect the sequences of experiment steps<
>- System messages and audio warning if the experiment step is skipped or executed in a wrong order<
>- Structured logs with time stamps and video streaming endpoint used for local storage<
>- A unique GUI without a need of internet connection<
>- Stretch goal: creating a 3D human mesh recovery system independent of orientation, thus providing pose tracking relatively to the rack with equipment instead of the fixed “up/down” direction – this is highly relevant since the area of experiment is microgravity environment<


### Biological and DNA level feedback for biometrical BCI systems (research collaboration)<br>
>The current research is in the initial stages of studying the possibility of using continuous feedback from the activity of human cell/DNA by means of the well-fitted neural network in training of biometrical BCI systems with higher accuracy than now.
